Common questions

Where are my games saved?

Your games are stored on your device. The app does not provide accounts or its own cloud sync, and the developer cannot retrieve games from your phone.

Can I keep more than one game going?

Yes. Choose Start new game from the main table, then tap any ongoing game to resume it.

How do I remove a game?

Open the game and choose Delete game. This removes the game and its rounds, including retained local backup copies. You can also end an ongoing game to keep its scores in the ledger without adding a round.

Can I use the app offline?

Yes. Scoring, saved games, and scoring help work offline. Opening this site or sending email requires a connection.

Does it include house rules?

The in-app Scoring guide describes the rules used by the scoreboard, including winning a trick to count meld and a moon shot requiring one player to take all 12 tricks.
