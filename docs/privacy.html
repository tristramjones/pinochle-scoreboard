Privacy policy

Pinochle Scoreboard
Effective November 24, 2026

The app stores game information on your device. It does not send your games to the developer and does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics.

Information stored by the app

Team names, game dates, bids, meld, trick points, rounds, and scores are saved locally so you can continue games and view your ledger. No account is required.

Collection and sharing

The app does not transmit game data to the developer, sell personal information, or share game information with advertising or analytics services. Scoring and scoring help work offline.

Retention, deletion, and device backups

Games remain on your device until you delete them. Open a game and choose Delete game to remove it and its rounds, including retained local backup copies. Deleting the app through iOS Settings also removes its local app data; offloading an app can retain its data.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may include app data in device backups. Apple manages those backups under its own privacy practices. The app does not operate its own cloud-sync service.

Support email

If you contact trisjones04@gmail.com, the developer receives the email address and message you choose to send and uses them to respond to your request. Email is handled by the email services involved and is separate from your locally stored games.

This website and external services

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, under its privacy statement. This site does not add analytics, advertising, cookies, or contact forms. Links to support and privacy information open your browser or email app, which have their own privacy practices.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised effective date.
